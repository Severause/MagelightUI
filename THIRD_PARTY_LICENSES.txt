Magelight UI - third-party license texts
========================================

Magelight.dll and MagelightGPU.dll are compiled with the libraries below, and
the example pages that ship with Magelight carry the JavaScript libraries at
the end of this file. Each entry names the component, where it ends up, and
its license text as its authors publish it. NOTICES.txt lists everything
Magelight ships, including the components whose terms travel in their own
files (Ultralight, the LGPL GPU driver, the Web Inspector).
